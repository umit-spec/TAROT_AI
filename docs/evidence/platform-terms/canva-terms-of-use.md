## Note on the "shared Design" perpetual license clause

The third clause above — a **perpetual** license triggered specifically
by **sharing** a Design with others — was not previously called out in
the FAZ9A-era terms summary, which recorded only the general storage
license. Whether any of the Canva design(s) holding the 79 card images
were ever "shared" (Canva's specific product term for enabling
collaborator/link access) rather than kept private is a factual question
only the product owner can answer, and could matter to whether an
additional perpetual license was granted to Canva beyond ordinary private
storage. Flagged as a §D packet question, not resolved here.
