# Aşama 2 — Persona Wireframe Paths

## Purpose

Same wireframe layout, 5 distinct reading experiences. This document defines how each persona's flow differs through the MERGED questions screen and subsequent reading displays, without changing UI layout.

---

## Persona 1: First-Time User

**Detection:** Answers indicate no tarot experience, curious tone, asks "what does this mean?"

**Wireframe Variations:**

### Questions Screen (25 sec merged)
- **Question 1:** "Ilk defa mı tarot okuyorsunuz?" (checkbox or visual)
- **Question 2:** "Bu süreci meraklı mı başlıyorsunuz, yoksa endişeli mi?" (emotional frame)
- **Question 3:** "Bu okuma size ne göstersin istiyorsunuz?" (open guidance)
- **Tone:** Warm, explanatory, use simpler words
- **Help text inline:** "Tarot kendi gücünüzü anlamanıza yardımcı olur" (small, unobtrusive)

### First Insight Display (12 sec)
- **Opening:** "İşte kartlarınız ve ilk anlamları:"
- **Card meaning:** Basic interpretation (1-2 sentences)
- **Relevance:** "Bu kartlar sorunuza neden önemli?"
- **Tone:** Encouraging, "This is telling you..."
- **Font size:** Slightly larger (readability for less experienced users)
- **Visual hint:** Underline or highlight key card name (recognition aid)

### Detailed Synthesis Display (25 sec)
- **Structure:** Card-by-card breakdown, then narrative
- **Language:** Explain symbolism ("This water represents emotions")
- **Word count:** ~250 words (longer, more educational)
- **Call-to-action:** "What one action could you take based on this insight?"
- **Reassurance:** "There's no 'wrong' interpretation; trust your intuition"

## Persona 2: Regular Practitioner

**Detection:** Mentions previous readings, familiar with spreads, confident language

**Wireframe Variations:**

### Questions Screen (25 sec merged)
- **Question 1:** "Rutin olarak okuma almış mısınız?" (checkbox)
- **Question 2:** "Bu okumada derinliğe mi yoksa hızlı rehberliğe mi ihtiyacınız var?" (depth preference)
- **Question 3:** "Aşinasınız mısınız beş kartlı açılımla?" (spread knowledge)
- **Tone:** Direct, assumes tarot literacy, no explanation needed
- **Help text:** Minimal or none

### First Insight Display (12 sec)
- **Opening:** Direct. "Kartlarınız:"
- **Card meaning:** Symbolic interpretation assuming knowledge of archetypal meanings
- **Relevance:** "This pattern suggests..."
- **Tone:** Respectful, peer-to-peer ("As you know...")
- **Visual hint:** Show card number/position (technical metadata)

### Detailed Synthesis Display (25 sec)
- **Structure:** Quick narrative arc with deeper patterns
- **Language:** Archetypal references, numerology, position psychology
- **Word count:** ~200 words (denser, more conceptual)
- **Call-to-action:** "How does this align with your broader question?"
- **Advanced note:** Mention energy patterns or shadow aspects if relevant

## Persona 3: Highly Anxious User

**Detection:** Uses words like "worried," "nervous," or selects anxiety-related topic; multiple hesitations

**Wireframe Variations:**

### Questions Screen (25 sec merged)
- **Question 1:** "Stres ya da kaygı konusu mu?" (direct validation)
- **Question 2:** "Bu okumada size rahat hissettirecek ne olurdu?" (agency, control)
- **Question 3:** "Neyin kontrolünde olduğunuza güvenmeniz önemli mi?" (empowerment frame)
- **Tone:** Warm, validating, reassuring
- **Help text:** "Bu güvenli bir alan; endişeleriniz önemlidir"
- **Color accent:** Soft warmth, not clinical

### First Insight Display (12 sec)
- **Opening:** Validating. "Korkularınız gerçek, ama buradaki harita:"
- **Card meaning:** Reframed as agency, not threat. ("This isn't saying something bad will happen; it's showing where your power lies")
- **Relevance:** "This reflects your care—and care is a strength."
- **Tone:** Protective, empowering, "You have agency here"
- **Visual design:** Avoid dark imagery, use warm lighting on cards
- **Reassurance sentence:** Always included. "There's no judgment here."

### Detailed Synthesis Display (25 sec)
- **Structure:** Problem + strength + action path
- **Language:** "You're doing great," "You're not alone," "This is manageable"
- **Word count:** ~180 words (shorter for cognitive ease; anxiety affects reading capacity)
- **Call-to-action:** "One small step you could take today?"
- **Closing:** "You have more control than you think."

## Persona 4: Decision-Maker

**Detection:** Asks specific yes/no questions, practical language ("What should I do?"), time-bound context

**Wireframe Variations:**

### Questions Screen (25 sec merged)
- **Question 1:** "Belirli bir kararı vermek zorunda mısınız?" (binary frame)
- **Question 2:** "Zaman baskısı var mı?" (timeline, urgency)
- **Question 3:** "İhtiyacınız olan ne: seçenekeleri anlamak mı, yoksa karar verme gücü mü?" (clarity vs. confidence)
- **Tone:** Business-like, efficient, no flowery language
- **Help text:** None; get to the point

### First Insight Display (12 sec)
- **Opening:** Actionable. "Kartlarınız hangi yönü gösteriyor:"
- **Card meaning:** Translated to decision factors. ("This position represents what's holding you back; this one shows opportunity.")
- **Relevance:** "Here's the path forward."
- **Tone:** Clear, directional, "Next step is..."
- **Visual:** Show spread positions clearly (which card = which factor?)

### Detailed Synthesis Display (25 sec)
- **Structure:** Situation → Options → Recommendation
- **Language:** Practical, risk/benefit language, avoid ambiguity
- **Word count:** ~200 words (concise, actionable)
- **Call-to-action:** "If you choose path A, watch for X. If you choose B, prepare for Y."
- **Closing:** "Trust your judgment; this gives you context to decide."

## Persona 5: Curious Skeptic

**Detection:** Mentions psychology, science, or says "but does this actually work?"; rational framing

**Wireframe Variations:**

### Questions Screen (25 sec merged)
- **Question 1:** "Tarot'un psikolojik açıdan nasıl çalıştığına ilgi mi duyuyorsunuz?" (science frame)
- **Question 2:** "Belirsizlikle baş etmede yardım mı arıyorsunuz, yoksa test mi etmek istiyorsunuz?" (intent clarity)
- **Question 3:** "Kendine özgü yorumlamaya mı yoksa veriye dayalı çıkarımlar mı daha yakın hissediyorsunuz?" (cognitive style)
- **Tone:** Intellectual, respects skepticism, no woo-woo
- **Help text:** "Tarot kaynağından ziyade kendi içgörünüzü aynaya tutar" (mirror, not source)

### First Insight Display (12 sec)
- **Opening:** Frame it scientifically. "İşte kartlarınız ve psikolojik yorumlar:"
- **Card meaning:** Psychological frame. ("This archetype often represents patterns we recognize in ourselves; apophenia is natural human cognition.")
- **Relevance:** "These symbols reflect common decision-making patterns; what does YOUR intuition say?"
- **Tone:** Respectful of reasoning, "This is a tool for reflection, not prediction"
- **Visual:** Subtle symbolism explanation ("Water = emotion, not literal")

### Detailed Synthesis Display (25 sec)
- **Structure:** Symbolism → Psychology → What this means for YOU
- **Language:** "Research shows...", "One interpretation could be...", "Your mind filled in..."
- **Word count:** ~220 words (intellectual depth)
- **Call-to-action:** "What patterns do you recognize? How might you test this insight?"
- **Closing:** "The value is what you extract, not the tool itself."

## Cross-Persona Notes

### Questions Screen (Same Layout, Different Copy)

**All personas:**
- 3 questions, merged into one natural conversation
- Persona detection algorithm runs silently in background
- User sees only questions, not "we're assessing you"
- Questions feel topic-specific + emotional, not a test
- Inline spread recommendation after questions (5 sec)

**Layout never changes:**
- Question appears (readable in ≤3 sec)
- User taps answer or types response
- Next question appears smoothly
- After Q3, spread recommendation ("System suggests 3-card spread based on your question. Accept? / Change to 5-card?")

### Reading Display (Same Layout, Different Tone)

**All personas:**
- First Insight section: Same structure, DIFFERENT LANGUAGE
  - First-timer: Warm, explanatory, larger text
  - Regular: Direct, symbolically dense, compact
  - Anxious: Validating, empowering, reassuring
  - Decision-maker: Actionable, risk-clear, directional
  - Skeptic: Psychological, evidence-based, reflects-not-predicts

- Detailed Synthesis section: Same space, DIFFERENT DEPTH + FRAMING
  - First-timer: Longer (250 words), educational tone
  - Regular: Shorter (200 words), advanced symbolism
  - Anxious: Shorter (180 words), agency-focused
  - Decision-maker: Compact (200 words), action-oriented
  - Skeptic: Intellectual (220 words), psychology-grounded

### Spread Recommendation (Same UI, Different Messaging)

**All personas see:**
- System suggests 3-card or 5-card based on topic + persona
- One button: "Accept" or "Change to 5-card / 3-card"
- Same visual affordance (card illustration, spread name)

**But messaging differs:**
- First-timer: "This spread is great for beginners. Want to try it?"
- Regular: "Recommended: 3-card for focus."
- Anxious: "3-card keeps it simple; you're in control."
- Decision-maker: "5-card shows all angles. Time to decide?"
- Skeptic: "3-card minimizes pattern-seeking bias."

## Testing Guidance for 5-User Validation

When testing wireframes with 5 users:

1. **User 1 (First-timer):** Selects "relationship" topic, shows hesitation
   - Expect: Longer read time due to explanatory text
   - Test: Does tone feel safe? Does user complete reading?

2. **User 2 (Regular):** Selects "mood" topic, confident answers
   - Expect: Faster read time due to compact text
   - Test: Does user feel respected? Do they skip sections?

3. **User 3 (Anxious):** Selects "general" topic, many hesitations
   - Expect: Longer navigation time due to reading reassurances
   - Test: Does tone reduce anxiety? Do they save the reading?

4. **User 4 (Decision-maker):** Selects "career" topic, very specific question
   - Expect: Quick navigation, reads action section carefully
   - Test: Do they understand decision implications? Is messaging clear?

5. **User 5 (Skeptic):** Selects "general" topic, asks "does this work?"
   - Expect: Reads explanation text, questions assumptions
   - Test: Do they feel the tool is honest? Do they appreciate psychology framing?

## Success Criteria for Persona Paths

✅ **Each persona's wireframe should:**
- Maintain same TIME BUDGET (80 sec to First Insight, 115 sec core flow)
- Use distinct TONE (copy, not layout, differs)
- Deliver different DEPTH (word count, structure)
- Show different VISUAL EMPHASIS (which elements stand out per persona)
- Follow same FUNNEL EVENTS (all 12 analytics events fired regardless of persona)

**PASS if:** 5-user test includes at least one user per persona type, and timing + clarity pass for each.
